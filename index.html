Arduino Ultrasonic Radar System

A servo-mounted ultrasonic sensor sweeps 180°, measures the distance to objects and warns with LEDs, a buzzer and an LCD, while a laptop draws the live radar screen.

How one measurement works

How the sweep finds objects

The servo turns one degree at a time. At each angle the sensor takes a reading, so the angle and distance together give an object’s position.

Complete circuit diagram

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram. Moving dots show signal direction. Red “5V” and dark “GND” tags are all joined through the breadboard power rails. Both 250 Ω resistors sit on the breadboard.
